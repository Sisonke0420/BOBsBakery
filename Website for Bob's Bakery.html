About Us

BOB’s Bakery is a family-owned favorite since 2003. We bake everything from scratch each morning — think hand-shaped sourdough, flaky honey croissants, and Bob's famous caramel buns. We source local ingredients, rotate seasonal specials, and cater community events across Bethlehem.


Testimony

Our bestsellers include the Caramel Bun, Rustic Sourdough, and Chocolate Hazelnut Tart. Other crowd-pleasers you should try: Almond Croissant, Cinnamon Swirl, Blueberry Muffins, Gluten-Free Banana Bread, and Savory Cheese Scones. Pair pastries with our house-blend coffee or grab a mixed pastry box for sharing. We avoid preservatives, offer vegan options, and provide custom orders for events — order ahead for pickup or same-day delivery in nearby neighborhoods. Ask about our subscription boxes and catering packages for recurring deliveries or special events.
